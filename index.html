Your trip, your flight and your layover. One app, built for crew.

CrewMate is an iPhone app for flight attendants and pilots. It's not a passenger flight tracker. It's built around your schedule, your report time and your rest.

Download on the App Store

Free to download. CrewMate Premium is $7.99 a month or $59.99 a year.

Free for every crew member

  • Your schedule: import trips from your calendar, or add them by hand, with your history and stats.
  • Rest tools: a rest legalities calculator and an in-flight rest planner.
  • Pilot logbook.
  • Commuting backup: Plan B, for when a commute falls through.
  • Layover tools: nearby places, currency, bill and tip splitter, unit converter.
  • Translator in 25 languages.
  • Emergency numbers and help, always free.
  • Jumpseat countdown, seniority tracking and private crew notes.

For the days that don't go to plan

  • What a delay means for you: CrewMate tells you how a flight change affects your duty, your rest and your day.
  • Your flight, live: status, timeline, route and turbulence ahead.
  • On your lock screen: follow your flight without opening the app.
  • "Will I make report?": commute readiness for your report time.
  • The inbound aircraft you're waiting on, plus a watchlist of other flights.
  • Ask Eddie: an assistant for layover questions.

Honest by design

Live information in CrewMate shows where it comes from and how fresh it is. When a source is missing or late, the app says so instead of guessing. CrewMate is a planning aid, not your airline's official system. Always check anything operational with your airline.

No third-party advertising and no tracking across other apps.
