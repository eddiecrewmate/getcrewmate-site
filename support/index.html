CrewMate support

CrewMate is a companion app for working airline crew: your schedule, duty time, live flight status and on-the-road tools.

Contact us

Email support@getcrewmate.app. For a flight question, please include the flight number and date. We read every message and reply by email.

CrewMate is a planning aid, not an official source. Always follow your airline, crew scheduling, dispatch and air traffic control. See our Terms of Use.

Delete your account

In the app, open Account (from the Toolkit tab, or the account button on Today) and tap Delete account at the bottom of the screen. Your account and the data stored with it are deleted. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it separately (below).

If you can no longer sign in, email support@getcrewmate.app from the address you used for CrewMate and ask us to delete your account.

Restore a subscription

If you subscribed and the app does not show your subscription (for example on a new iPhone), open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. To change or cancel, on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose CrewMate. You can also follow Apple's guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple. Refund requests go to Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
